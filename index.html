Community Bulletin

The city council has announced a new season of neighborhood programs focused on recreation, public safety, and local volunteer opportunities. Residents can join weekend cleanups, attend open-air concerts, and visit pop-up information desks at participating community centers.

Registration support will be available during weekday office hours for anyone who needs help choosing an event or updating contact information. Event coordinators also recommend checking the portal before attending, since weather updates and venue changes will be posted online first.

Community groups are invited to submit announcements for future bulletins. Notices should include the event name, location, date, contact details, and a short description so residents can quickly understand how to participate.
